# Notebooks Overview 📚

## [01_intro](01_intro.ipynb)

Introduces basic operations like loading, displaying, and saving images using OpenCV.  
Highlights color spaces and simple plotting with Matplotlib.

## [02_point](02_point.ipynb) & [02_point_HW](02_point_HW.ipynb)

Covers point operations, including LUT transformations, arithmetic operations, and logical operations on images.  
Homework explores bit-plane slicing and reconstructing images from subsets of bits.

## [03_histogram](03_histogram.ipynb) & [03_histogram_hw](03_histogram_hw.ipynb)

Explores histogram-based transformations, equalization, and stretching. Homework delves deeper into histogram analysis.

## [04_thresholding](04_thresholding.ipynb) & [04_thresholding_hw](04_thresholding_hw.ipynb)

Discusses techniques for binarizing images using global and local thresholds, Otsu’s method, and more.

## [05_resolution](05_resolution.ipynb) & [05_resolution_hw](05_resolution_hw.ipynb)

Focuses on image resizing, interpolation methods, and resolution scales.

## [06_context](06_context.ipynb)

Looks at context-based image transformations and filtering operations.

## [06_context_hw1](06_context_hw1.ipynb) & [06_context_hw2](06_context_hw2.ipynb)

Homework on median filtering for color images and on adaptive median filtering.
